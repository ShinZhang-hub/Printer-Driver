Booklet Trimming

The fore edge that is on the opposite side of the bi-folded paper spine and the top and bottom edges will be trimmed. The spine is the binding edge of a bi-folded paper.


Trim size

Specify [Width], [Height], and [Adjust Trim Position] of the trim size from the spine to the fore edge of the paper after trimming.
The trim size can be specified in increments of 0.1mm or in increments of 0.01inch. You can either use the keyboard or the scroll button to specify the trim size. The trim size may vary depending on the paper size.


Width

Specify the width of the paper after trimming.

Height

Specify the height of the paper after trimming.

Adjust Trim Position

Specify the trim position.

Units

Select the unit of measurement from millimeters and inches.
