Booklet Trimming

The fore edge opposite the spine of the bi-folded paper will be trimmed. The spine is the binding edge of a bi-folded paper.

Width

Specify the width from the spine to the fore edge of the paper after trimming.
The width can be specified in increments of 0.1mm or in increments of 0.01inch. You can either use the keyboard or the scroll button to specify the value.
The width may vary depending on the paper size.
Available paper sizes for [Trimmer] are A3, A4, B4, Letter (8.5 x 11") and Tabloid (11 x 17").
